# Welcome to HyperAI 🙌🏻

[Notebooks](https://hyper.ai/notebooks) | [Datasets](https://hyper.ai/datasets) | [Wiki](https://hyper.ai/wiki)

[HyperAI](https://hyper.ai/) is a search engine dedicated to data science. We strive to become the future infrastructure for the data science, delivering tangible value to practitioners across the industry. To date, we have provided:

* **1,200+** Download mirrors for large-scale public datasets
* **300+** Encyclopedia entries related to Artificial Intelligence
* **200+** Articles and insights focused on real-world AI applications

Moving forward, we are introducing **online GPU computing resources** specifically for researchers and developers. We will also continue to release more materials related to data science and machine learning compilers, aiming to serve a broader developer community and deliver even greater value!

The HyperAI community adheres to the principles of open-source collaboration. We welcome developers to join us and contribute to the ecosystem!
